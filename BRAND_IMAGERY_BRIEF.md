# Brand imagery brief

A brief for whoever generates images for the LMAA Family App — a person or an agent running Nano Banana 2 through Higgsfield's web app. It says what the app actually needs, exactly how big, one prompt per shot, and how to tell a good result from a bad one.

Everything here is anchored in the academy's real room, taken from its own photographs: **blue interlocking mats with a red border, white walls, floor-to-ceiling storefront glass with the parking lot and trees outside, a low white shelf of red, blue and yellow kick pads under the windows, and the Korean flag high on the far wall.** Consistency comes from repeating that room, not from a filter.

## Why these images, and why no people

The app is text-first by design and already looks like the academy. Images do two jobs: they make an **event or update header** feel like it came from this school rather than a template, and they give the **launch posts** something to stand on. Neither needs a person in frame.

Generated children are off the table — this is a children's academy, and that is precisely why. Generated faces of any kind look wrong on close inspection and would stand in for real people who exist. So every shot is the room, the kit and the objects of training: belts, uniforms, pads, boards, shoes at the mat edge, at most an adult's hands. Those read as more honest _and_ more real.

The things that must stay real stay real: the **front door and logo decal**, the **instructors**, and the **photo gallery** are for the academy's own photographs only.

## How to run it

- **Model.** Nano Banana 2 on the unlimited tier only. Do not spend credits. Check the plan screen before the first generation.
- **Aspect ratio.** Set per shot (listed on each card). Do not crop afterwards; generate at the ratio.
- **Resolution.** The highest the tier allows, at least 1600 px on the long side for headers and 1080 × 1920 for the story.
- **Variations.** Three or four per shot. Pick with the checklist; do not pick the prettiest.
- **Reference images.** If the tool accepts reference images, give it two or three of the academy's own room photos from leesmartialartsacademy.com (for example images/class-seated.jpg and images/prog-adults.jpg) with the instruction: match this room's mats, walls, windows and shelf exactly; do not copy the people. That is the single biggest lever for consistency.
- **Prompt order.** Paste the universal style block first, a blank line, then the shot prompt. Every shot, every time.
- **Files.** Save each pick with the exact filename on its card, full resolution, PNG or best-quality JPEG. Put them in one folder.

## The universal style block

Paste this first, before every shot prompt, unchanged.

```text
Documentary photograph, not an illustration or render. Location: a real, modest, well-kept Taekwondo school in a suburban Oregon strip-mall unit. Interior details that must stay consistent: blue interlocking foam mats with a red border strip around the edge; plain white walls; floor-to-ceiling storefront windows along one side with soft overcast daylight, a parking lot and green trees faintly visible outside; a low white shelf under the windows stacked with red, blue and yellow kicking pads; a ballet-style wooden barre along the window wall; a large Korean flag high on the far wall, soft and out of focus. Late afternoon. Natural window light plus neutral white ceiling panels, no coloured gels. Shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera, 35mm lens, f/2.8, ISO 800, slight natural grain, true-to-life colour, white uniforms actually white, no HDR, no heavy retouching, no cinematic teal-and-orange grade, framing slightly imperfect as if taken by a local newspaper photographer. Small honest details are welcome: a scuff on a mat, a water bottle, a strip of tape on the floor. Absolutely no people's faces and no children anywhere in frame; at most an adult's hands, feet or back, cropped. No readable text, lettering, logos, numbers or flag detail. No watermark.
```

## Picking a result

Run every candidate through this before saving it:

- [ ] No faces, no children, no recognisable person. Hands, feet or a back are the most that may appear.
- [ ] No readable text, numbers, logos or flag detail anywhere, including on boxes, boards or paper.
- [ ] The room is right: blue mats with a red border, white walls, storefront windows with daylight, the kick-pad shelf. Not a gym, not a studio with black curtains.
- [ ] White uniforms are white, not cream, grey or blue.
- [ ] Nothing impossible: belts tied in ways that cannot exist, melted pads, six fingers, boards floating, shadows going two directions.
- [ ] The subject sits inside the middle band of the frame for headers; nothing important near the top or bottom edge.
- [ ] It looks like a photo a local newspaper would run, not a product render: slight grain, honest light, a little mess.
- [ ] Pick the calmest of the variations. The app is quiet; a busy image fights the text on top of it.

## Do not generate

- Children or anyone's face. This is a children's academy, which is exactly why we do not generate children.
- The storefront, the front door or the logo decal. Those exist; a generated version would be a lie about what the building looks like. A real photo goes there.
- Instructors. Master Lee and the team are real people; nothing generated stands in for them.
- The photo gallery in the app. It is for the academy's own photographs, with permission, and nothing else.
- Text in images, including on belts, boards, certificates and the Korean flag's detail — the model mangles it and it dates instantly.
- The words beautiful, stunning, cinematic, epic, 8k, hyperdetailed, masterpiece. They push the result toward the glossy AI look we are avoiding.

## Notes on specific shots

- **The belt journey.** The academy's belt order is white, yellow, orange, green, blue, brown, red, black, with a senior stripe at the halfway point of each colour. The stripe's colour has not been confirmed, so the prompt leaves stripes out. Do not add them unless Master Lee confirms what they look like.
- **Testing reminder and the paddle shot** are the only two with any human body in frame. If the hands or foot look wrong in every variation, drop the shot; `update-general.jpg` works as a fallback header.
- **Launch backgrounds.** Leave the top third genuinely empty. A real screenshot of the app and the headline in the app's own type go there afterwards, so the UI shown is always the real UI.
